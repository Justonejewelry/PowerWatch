🧭 How the score works

Who's buying

CEO / CFO purchases weigh most, then other officers, directors, and 10% owners. Rank is knowledge — the closer to the top, the more they know.

How much

Bigger personal checks score higher. A $1M open-market buy is a statement; a $3K buy is a shrug.

Clusters

Two or more insiders at the same company buying in the same window multiplies the signal. One buyer can be wrong — a crowd rarely buys together by accident.

Offsets

Heavy non-planned insider selling at the same company cuts the score. Planned 10b5-1 sales are excluded — they're scheduled in advance and say little.
